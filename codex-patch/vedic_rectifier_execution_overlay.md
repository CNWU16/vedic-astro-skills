## Canonical method: judgment first

- Every item (dated events against Dasha; facts, traits, experiences against
  candidate-varying structures) goes into one mapping table with a judged
  ✅✅ / ✅ / ⚠️ / ❌ and a reason for every candidate. Group items explained by
  the same chart structure and weigh each group once.
- Settle with the main-line judgment, a one-time runner-up advocacy, a
  confidence level, and the decisive or most discriminating items, as the
  skill's "每层统一流程" requires. The reference score is shown; if it runs
  opposite to the judgment, state why.
- Only decisive opposing evidence (user-confirmed objective fact or dated
  event that one candidate strongly predicts and the other predicts the
  opposite of) may settle a pair on its own. Structural highlights,
  self-assessed traits, boundary events, and post-hoc rules never decide alone.
- At fine-tuning layers, do not reflexively rerun Dasha. Follow the skill's
  candidate-varying MD/AD or qualifying day-precision PD condition.

## Always-on execution discipline

- Preserve the authorized time range and complete legal candidate set. A poor
  fit never expands the range or deletes a candidate without operator authority.
- Apply the same evidence, chart source, and method to every candidate. Do not
  deepen only the current favorite or use UC to decide which candidate receives
  attention.
- Keep `current leader`, `current best estimate`,
  `active-protocol confirmation`, `user-adopted working chart`,
  `time confidence`, and `post-feedback fit` distinct whenever two could be
  confused. Do not force all labels into client prose when the distinction is
  irrelevant.
- Do not create a second total beside the skill's displayed reference score.
- New evidence triggers equal-method review across all applicable candidates.
  Invalidate only rows or conclusions affected by corrected evidence, an
  invalid chart/Dasha source, or an applicable skill/operator rule.

## Settle before asking

- Before requesting another fact, date, event, trait, or questionnaire, use
  all already-authorized material to complete the current layer's mapping
  table and judgment.
- High or medium confidence: present the interim conclusion and stop for the
  user's confirmation. Do not keep collecting proof.
- Low confidence: read `resources/calibration_quiz.md` before asking. Directly
  ask a missing concrete fact or dated event when it can discriminate;
  otherwise lock and send one round of candidate-pair questions.
- After each round, apply every valid answer to all candidates and re-judge.
  At most two rounds per layer; if still low, give the best estimate and what
  evidence is missing, and let the user decide. Never declare the layer
  closed on your own, and never make external records a prerequisite.

## Visibility without ritual

- Show every table, reference score, calculation, lock, and rationale that `SKILL.md`
  or `calibration_quiz.md` requires. Supplemental audit material may live in
  the named artifact when the skill does not require it verbatim in chat.
- In chat, show the candidate difference that matters, the strongest support,
  strongest counterevidence, settlement, and next transition. Do not recite
  unchanged ledgers or the entire protocol after every incremental answer.
- Final client-facing explanation also follows `vedic_client_voice.md`: clear
  astrological judgment first, one proportionate limitation where material,
  and no repetitive disclaimer language.
